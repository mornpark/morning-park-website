Intelligent Operations

Custom AI built into the tools your team already uses.

These are not off-the-shelf subscriptions. Each system is designed around your specific workflows, integrated with your existing software, and fully documented so you own it completely.

Sole Operator — Small Portfolio

For independent landlords and property managers overseeing up to 50 units. One automated workflow — typically maintenance triage, rent follow-up, or tenant communications — built and live within two weeks. No new software to learn. Runs quietly in the background while you focus on your properties.

  • Single AI-powered workflow designed for your operation
  • SMS-based — tenants use their regular phone, no app needed
  • You own the phone number, the data, and the system
  • Plain-English documentation included

Growing Portfolio — 50 to 300 Units

For property management companies managing dozens to hundreds of units, where manual coordination is costing real hours every week. Multi-workflow automation integrated directly with AppFolio or Buildium — maintenance triage, vendor dispatch, follow-up sequences, and a coordinator dashboard. Built in three to four weeks.

  • Two to three connected workflows running as one system
  • Direct integration with AppFolio or Buildium
  • Coordinator alert system — exceptions only, no noise
  • Live analytics dashboard and monthly report
  • All data stays in your accounts

Full Operation — 300+ Units

For established property management companies where operational efficiency is a competitive advantage. A fully custom-scoped build covering your highest-volume pain points — from maintenance and tenant communications to owner reporting and vendor management. Dedicated account support and complete succession documentation so a new developer can take over at any time.

  • Custom scope based on your specific workflows and team
  • Enterprise integrations (AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, and others)
  • Complete system handoff documentation
  • Dedicated support and quarterly system review
  • Built under NDA and Data Processing Agreement

All systems are built with a pilot arrangement available for first engagements. Pricing is scoped to your portfolio size and integration requirements — discussed on your discovery call.
