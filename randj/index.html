Do you do smog checks?

Yes. R & J is a California Smog Check station, with emissions testing and repair on site.

Will my car pass smog with the check engine light on?

No. In California a car won't pass a Smog Check with the check engine light on. Get the light diagnosed and fixed first. A passing certificate goes to the DMV electronically and is good for 90 days.
