Smog check · Auburn, CA

Smog check here. If it fails, we fix it here too.

A smog-only station can tell you that you failed. It can't tell you why, and it can't fix it. We can, because we're a repair shop.

What to bring

01

Your DMV renewal notice

It says whether a smog check is required this year. Bring it, or a photo of it.

02

A little time

A passing certificate is good for 90 days, so don't leave it to the last week of your registration.

03

No check-engine light

A car won't pass a Smog Check with the check-engine light on. Call us first and we'll find out why, then test it.

If it fails

We find out why, tell you what the fix costs, and you decide. No second trip to another shop.

Common questions

How much is a smog check?

Call and we'll tell you the current price on the phone. We don't hide it; we just don't want a stale number sitting on a web page.

Do I need an appointment?

Call ahead. It gets you a slot instead of a wait, and we can tell you the price at the same time.

How long does it take?

Ask when you call — it depends how many cars are ahead of you that day.

Can you send the result to the DMV?

Yes. A passing certificate is sent to the DMV electronically. You'll still get a printed copy.
