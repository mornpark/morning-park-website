Questions

Smog check questions.

How much is a smog check at R & J?

Call (530) 885-0508 and we'll tell you the current price on the phone. We just don't want a stale number sitting on a web page.

Will my car pass smog with the check engine light on?

No. In California a car won't pass a Smog Check with the check engine light on. Get the light diagnosed and fixed first. We can do both.

How long is a smog certificate good for?

A passing certificate is good for 90 days, and it goes to the DMV electronically.

Do I need an appointment for a smog check?

Call ahead. It gets you a time instead of a wait, and we can tell you the price at the same time.

What if my car fails smog?

R & J is a repair shop as well as a Smog Check station. We find out why it failed, tell you what the fix costs, and you decide. No second trip to another shop.

What should I bring?

Your DMV renewal notice, or a photo of it. It says whether a smog check is required this year.
