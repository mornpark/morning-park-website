Privacy

How we handle your information.

Effective October 27, 2026

This notice covers randjauto.com, the website of Care 4 Cars Inc, doing business as R & J Automotive, 475 Grass Valley Hwy, Auburn, CA 95603.

What we collect

Only what you choose to type into our "tell us what's going on" form:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • What your car needs and how soon

If you just browse the site, we don't ask for anything, and the site doesn't set cookies. The only thing it remembers is whether you pressed "Pause motion," and that's stored in your own browser, not sent to us.

How we use it

To call you back about your car. That's it. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

Who receives it

  • R & J Automotive, by email.
  • Morning Park, the company that runs this website for the shop, to make sure messages arrive.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), the service that delivers the form to our email. Its own privacy policy applies to how it handles messages.

Other services the site uses

  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on this site. Your browser fetches it from Google, which sees your IP address the way any website does.
  • Google Maps loads only if you press "Show the map" on the Contact page. Google's own privacy policy applies to the map.
  • Our web host keeps standard server logs (like your IP address and the pages requested) to keep the site running and secure.

We don't use advertising trackers. Apart from Google's fonts and the map you choose to load, we don't let other companies collect information about you on this site.

"Do Not Track"

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because this site doesn't track you across other websites, it works the same whether or not that signal is on.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your information

Call the shop at (530) 885-0508, Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, and ask. We'll tell you what we have, correct it, or delete it from our records. Copies held by the form service follow that service's own policy.

Children

This site is for people arranging car repairs. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
