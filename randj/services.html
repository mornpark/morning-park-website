Questions

Straight answers.

Do you work on trucks, diesels and motor homes?

Yes. Along with cars, R & J does truck repair, minor diesel repair, and motor-home and RV repair.

What makes do you work on?

Most makes. CARFAX service history shows Chevrolet, Ford, Honda, Subaru and Toyota among the makes R & J services most, plus many others.

Will you tell me the price before you start?

Yes. We'll tell you what's wrong and what it costs before we start.

Is there a price list?

No. Every car is different. Call (530) 885-0508 with the year, the make and what it's doing, and we'll tell you what we can on the phone.

Do you do oil changes and factory scheduled maintenance?

Yes: oil changes, factory scheduled maintenance, filters, fluids, tune-ups and vehicle inspections.
