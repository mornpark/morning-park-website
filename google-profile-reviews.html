The fine print, in plain words

Payment starts the work

Signing this page and paying today is what starts our work together. Until then we don't begin work or ask you for logins, manager invites or access.

The hand-over list

Within 7 days of signing, you send us: a manager invite to your Google Business Profile (or the details to create one in your name), your logo, job photos, your services and service area, and how your team marks a job done. Setup timelines are counted from the day the list is complete.

Your monthly service has a fixed date

Monthly billing begins today and repeats on the same day each month. That date does not move if something on your side is late, including logins, approvals, photos, or anything a previous vendor still has to release. We work on everything that isn't blocked and pick up the rest as it arrives. If a delay is our fault, we extend your service by the same number of days at no charge.

Staying in touch

We reply to you within 2 business days, and we ask the same of you. If we're waiting on you for more than 10 business days, we may pause only the work that depends on your answer, and we'll tell you in writing. Everything else continues, and so does monthly billing. You can end the service at any time as described below.

No promises on results

Morning Park can't promise a number of reviews, a Google ranking, calls, jobs or revenue. Customers decide whether to leave a review, and Google decides what it shows. You pay for the work Morning Park does, not for results.

What you own

You own your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your texting number and your customer list. Every account is set up in your business's name.   • Morning Park uses customer contact information only for this service, never sells or shares it, and deletes or returns it within 14 days after the service ends.

Limits

Morning Park isn't responsible for decisions made by Google, phone carriers or other platforms, such as a removed review, a suspended profile or blocked texts. If something Morning Park does causes you a loss, the most Morning Park owes is what you paid in the 3 months before it happened.

Ending the service

  • To cancel, either side gives 30 days' notice by text or email. There's no cancellation fee. The monthly charge stops after the notice period.
  • The setup fee is refundable only under the promise above.
