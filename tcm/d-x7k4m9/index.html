How This Demo Works
1
Pick a demo tenant

This building has five demo units pre-configured. Click a row to call as that tenant. Write down their unit ID — the receptionist will ask for it.

2
Select the issue they're reporting

Pick one issue from the grid below. The routing visualization will reflect whatever you choose.

3
Optional: test the vendor fallback

Click "Make next vendor unavailable" before calling. The system will treat the primary vendor as unreachable and route to the backup instead.

4
Call the receptionist line, or run the demo sequence

Dial the number and go through the intake live. Or click "Run Demo Sequence" to watch the routing play out on screen without making a call.
