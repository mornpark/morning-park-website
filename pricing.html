Common questions

Do I need all three?

No. Most people start with one. The Assessment tells you which one, or whether you need any of them.

I already have a website person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Google Profile + Reviews works alongside whatever site you have. I don't replace people who are doing good work.

Why do the review requests go to every customer, not just the happy ones?

Google doesn't allow screening out unhappy customers, and it doesn't allow paying for reviews. Asking everyone keeps your profile safe. Most customers who had a good job are glad to help when it takes one tap.

Does the $999 count toward a build?

Yes. If you start any paid work within 90 days, the Assessment fee comes off your first invoice.

What does "see yours before you buy" mean?

If you don't have a website, I can build a private preview of one from your public Google listing so you can see it before you decide. It stays private and comes down after 7 days unless you want it.
