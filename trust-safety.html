Trust, Safety & Backup

We build systems that don't vanish when something goes wrong.

Every automation we deploy is documented, backed up, and recoverable. You should be able to see exactly what's running — and know it'll still run tomorrow.

How we operate
🔐
You own everything.

Every workflow, every contact, every automation lives in your GHL account — not ours. If we stopped working together tomorrow, your system keeps running.

📋
Every build is documented.

We don't build black boxes. Every workflow we create comes with a plain-English explanation of what it does, what triggers it, and how to turn it off.

🔄
Backup before change.

Before any edit to a live workflow, we export a working copy. Changes are tested in staging before touching your production automations.

👁
No quiet failures.

We monitor active automations and surface failures before they compound. If something breaks, you get notified — not a mystery silence.

📵
We don't hold access hostage.

No proprietary portals you can't access. No credentials only we control. Your accounts, your logins, your system. We're operators, not gatekeepers.

🧱
Built on stable platforms.

GHL, Claude, and Vercel aren't startups with runway concerns. The foundation your automations sit on is durable, well-funded, and widely adopted.

Our stack

GoHighLevel
CRM + Automation Layer

All contact management, workflow automation, scheduling, and messaging runs through GHL. SOC 2 compliant, GDPR-aware, and built specifically for service businesses.

Claude (Anthropic)
AI Engine

Claude handles the language layer — reading, drafting, classifying, and responding. Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach means it has guardrails built into how it reasons, not bolted on after.

Vercel
Hosting + Delivery

Static assets and serverless functions deploy on Vercel's global edge network. 99.99% uptime SLA, automatic SSL, and instant rollback if a deployment goes sideways.

What happens if…

What if Morning Park stops operating?
Your system keeps running. Everything lives in your accounts. We'll provide a full handoff document and 30 days of support if you want to transition management.
What if a workflow breaks and nobody notices?
We run monitoring on active automations. If a GHL workflow fails to trigger or a webhook goes silent, it flags in our dashboard within the hour.
What if I want to change something you built?
You have admin access to your own GHL account. We recommend flagging changes so we can stage them safely, but you're never locked out.
What data do you actually see?
We see whatever's in your GHL account as operators. We don't store or export your client data elsewhere. No third-party data resale, ever.
What if I need to leave quickly?
We'll remove our operator access within 24 hours of your request. No fees, no negotiation. A system handoff doc goes to you the same day.
