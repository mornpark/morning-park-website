Refunds, and the fine print

Can I get the deposit back?

Yes, in full, if you change your mind before the build starts. Once the build starts, the deposit covers the work done.

How do I stop the monthly service?

Either of us can end it with 30 days written notice. No fees.

What about build payments if I stop early?

Payments already made aren't refunded once the build has started. If you end things before month three, the build payments still owed are due only if your website is live.

Will texts to my own cell show up?

No. Only texts to the new number go into the system. Moving your own number over is a later conversation.

When can the new number start texting?

It needs approval from the phone carriers first, which can take days to weeks. Calls work sooner. No texting start date is promised.
