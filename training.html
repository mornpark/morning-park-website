Training

Own the system. Don’t just rent it.

Training is how we transfer what we built to your team, so you hold the keys — not us. Available at Foundation tier and above.

Foundation tier and above
01
Foundation Training
How the tools work, explained for real people.

The first sessions are about orientation: what each tool does, what to check daily, and how to handle the exceptions that will inevitably come up. Built for people who are not technical and don’t want to be — just want to know what’s running their business.

Foundation tier
02
Workshop Deep Dive
Full walkthrough. Every automation explained.

We go through every workflow, every automation trigger, and every decision point in the system you’re running. Your team leaves knowing not just what happens, but why — and how to modify it when your business changes.

Workshop tier
03
Operator Handoff
You run it. We’re available when it needs upgrading.

At the Operator level, the goal is full independence. Your team handles day-to-day operations of the system. We stay available for strategic upgrades, new integrations, and anything that requires rebuilding a piece — not as a crutch, but as a backstop.
