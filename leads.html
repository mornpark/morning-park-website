Lead Ownership
You own your pipeline. Not us.

Shared lead programs make you compete against five other businesses for the same contact. We build you the infrastructure to attract and capture your own leads — so every inquiry is exclusive to you, not auctioned to the highest bidder.

02
Qualification & Routing
Only qualified leads reach you.

Volume is not the goal. A plumber doesn’t need 50 leads a week — they need 10 good ones. We build the intake filters and routing logic so that leads are pre-qualified before they touch you: right service, right area, right budget signal.

03
Intake Scaling
The system grows without adding overhead.

Whether you’re handling 5 leads a week or 50, the infrastructure runs the same way — automated follow-up, CRM tagging, and pipeline stages that don’t require you to add staff just to keep up with demand.
