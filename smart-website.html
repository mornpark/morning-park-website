What This Is

We built ours first.
Here's what went into it.

A smart website isn't a different kind of website. It's a regular website that has had the right pieces added to it — pieces that capture leads, answer questions, route calls, and keep working on nights and weekends without you touching anything.

This page walks through every feature on mparklab.com and explains what it does and why it's there. Each one is available as a standalone add-on or part of a full build.

You're looking at the live example right now.
Lead Capture

The contact page asks questions instead of just taking a message

Most contact forms collect a name and email and stop there. This one runs a short quiz — what kind of business, what's hurting, how urgent, what budget. By the time someone finishes, JP knows enough to respond usefully. The quiz also routes people to the right page instead of leaving them to figure it out.

Phone Handling

One phone number routes every call without you picking up

The TCM (Tenant Call Management) prototype is a phone receptionist built for apartment buildings. A tenant calls, gets identified, describes the issue, and the call gets routed to the right vendor based on priority — emergency, urgent, or routine. A record of every call is logged. Nothing lands on your personal phone unless it has to.

Conversion Tool

A calculator shows visitors exactly what their problem is costing them

Instead of asking someone to trust that this is worth it, the ROI page lets them put in their own numbers. How many calls a week, close rate, average job value. The calculator shows the monthly cost of the problem in their own business. People who see that number convert at a much higher rate than people who read about it.

Voice Receptionist

A phone line that answers, qualifies, and books — without a person

The voice receptionist on this site picks up calls after hours, confirms who's calling, asks what they need, and routes or logs accordingly. For a local business this means no more missed calls at 7pm, no more "I'll call you back" messages that never get returned. The call either gets handled or gets logged for JP to follow up.
