Questions people ask

What does Big Country Renovation & Landscaping do?

Big Country is a Kauai company that does hauling, remodeling, demolition, construction, renovation and landscaping, including clearing overgrown land with a mini excavator and hauling the debris away.

Can Big Country clear an overgrown yard, lot or stream bank on Kauai?

Yes. Big Country clears brush, fallen branches and downed trees with a mini excavator. The before-and-after above shows a stream bank they cleared.

Does Big Country haul away the debris?

Yes. Hauling is one of Big Country's core services. Cleared brush and logs are loaded and taken off the property.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text Nico at (808) 720-9075, or fill in the short form at the top of the page with your name, phone, what you need and where on Kauai the job is.

Does Big Country work on all of Kauai?

Big Country is based on Kauai. Call (808) 720-9075 with your town to confirm they can reach your job.
