What's included

1. Review requests after every job

When a job is marked done, the customer gets a text from California Flat Roofs that says thanks and links straight to your Google review page. If they haven't left a review after 2 days, they get one friendly reminder. Then it stops. Nobody gets pestered.

2. The same ask for everyone

Every customer gets the same request. No one is screened out or steered away from posting a review anywhere, which follows Google's rules. If a customer says something went wrong, you reach out to make it right, and that customer still gets the same request.

3. Your own texting number, registered for you

Texts come from a number set up in California Flat Roofs' name. Morning Park handles the phone carrier registration (called A2P) in your business name. The number and carrier fees are included in the monthly price. Carrier approval can take days to weeks, so no start date is promised for the texting part.

4. A ping when a review comes in

You get a notice when a new Google review arrives, with a suggested reply you can OK. Replies to negative reviews go out only after you OK them.

5. Fitted to how you close out jobs

Justin sets up the "job done" step to match how your crew already finishes a job: a tap, a text, or the software you already use.

The fine print, in plain words

No promises on results

Morning Park can't promise a number of reviews, a Google ranking, calls, jobs or revenue. Customers decide whether to leave a review. California Flat Roofs pays for the work Morning Park does, not for results.

How we work together

  • Justin runs the setup and keeps it working. Questions by text or email get a reply within 2 business days.
  • Your part: mark jobs done, and answer Justin's setup questions within 2 business days. If something Justin needs is late, the dates that depend on it move back by the same amount.

What you own

California Flat Roofs owns its Google Business Profile, its reviews, its texting number and its customer list. If you leave, the texting account can be transferred to you or another provider, or exported, within 14 days at no charge. Morning Park's general know-how and templates stay Morning Park's.

Customer messages and data

  • Texts go out in California Flat Roofs' name, and only to customers who hired you. California Flat Roofs confirms its customers agreed to be contacted about their job, and is responsible for that consent.
  • Every text includes a way to stop further texts, and stop requests are honored.
  • Morning Park uses customer contact information only for this service, never sells or shares it, and deletes or returns it within 14 days after the service ends.

Limits

Morning Park isn't responsible for decisions made by Google, phone carriers or other platforms, such as a removed review or blocked texts. If something Morning Park does causes California Flat Roofs a loss, the most Morning Park owes is what California Flat Roofs paid in the 3 months before it happened.

Ending the service

  • Month to month. No minimum term.
  • To cancel, either side gives 30 days' notice by text or email. There's no cancellation fee. The monthly charge stops after the notice period.
  • The setup fee is refundable only under the 30-day promise above.
