What's included

1. Your website and Google profile, built and connected

A full website built from your real reviews and job photos, on your domain. Your Google Business Profile completed and pointed at the new site: the website button, your number (279) 250-4104, your real business name, categories, services, towns served, hours and job photos. Morning Park is added as a Manager. HB Trees stays the Owner.

2. Your whole social media system, set up for you

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Google posts. Morning Park sets up the accounts in HB Trees' name and runs the posting. There's no software for you to buy or learn. You or the crew shoot clips on a phone, Morning Park turns them into finished posts, and nothing posts without your OK.

3. Crew shoot ideas for every job

Before a scheduled job, you get a short shoot idea for the crew, two to five sentences: what to film, from where, and when. The ideas come from Morning Park's review of what the most-watched tree videos do, plus formats built on the homeowner's point of view, which none of the top videos use. HB Trees gets the property owner's OK before job photos or videos of their property are posted.

4. Review requests after every job

When a job wraps, the customer gets a short request to review HB Trees, with one reminder if they haven't responded, and then it stops. Every customer gets the same request. No one is screened out or discouraged from posting a review anywhere. If a customer says something went wrong, Tyler reaches out to make it right, and that customer still gets the same request. Every new Google review gets a reply in your voice, and replies to negative reviews go out only after you OK them.

5. One place for every lead

Calls, forms and messages land in one place, and the person reaching out gets a quick text reply. Texting needs carrier registration, which can take days to weeks, so no start date is promised for the texting part.

6. Jobber, your choice

Your jobs, scheduling, quotes and invoices stay in Jobber exactly as they are. For the review step, you choose: Morning Park connects Jobber so finished jobs send the review request, or Morning Park's system handles that step on its own. Justin helps set it up either way.

At launch, done once, as two separate messages

  • Past customers who were never asked get an honest review request. No discount or gift is offered for a review.
  • Referrals: a separate friends-and-family message with a limited-time discount you choose. It is never tied to leaving a review.

Both go by email, or by text only to people who have agreed to get texts from HB Trees.

The fine print, in plain words

No promises on results

Morning Park can't promise that HB Trees will get any new business from this work, and it can't say how much business it might bring in. Any numbers that come up, including HB Trees' own job prices, are examples to think with, not predictions. Morning Park does not promise a number of reviews, followers or views, Google rankings, AI-search placement, calls, leads, jobs or revenue. HB Trees pays for the work Morning Park does, not for results.

How we work together

  • Justin runs the work. Morning Park decides how and when the work gets done: the tools, the order, the posting schedule and the setup details. Things are adjusted to fit how HB Trees runs its business.
  • Justin keeps you posted. You get a short update from Justin each month on what was done and what's next, so there's no need to chase him. Questions by text or email get a reply within 2 business days. Morning Park isn't an on-call service.
  • Your part. Justin will reach out when he needs something: clips from a job, an OK on a post, access to an account, a quick answer. Please reply within 2 business days. If something Justin needs is late, the dates that depend on it move back by the same amount, and the monthly price doesn't change.

What you own, and the logins

HB Trees owns its domain, website, Google Business Profile, social media accounts, reviews, photos, videos and customer list.

  • Set up in your name. Justin creates or finishes each account in HB Trees' name, using an HB Trees email address, and adds himself as an admin or manager.
  • You get the keys. As each account is ready, and no later than 30 days after the build ends, Justin hands HB Trees the owner login and password. Keep them somewhere safe. Justin keeps his own admin login to do the work.
  • Please don't lock Justin out mid-service. Changing a password or removing his access is your right at any time. If it happens while the service is running, that part of the work pauses until access is back.
